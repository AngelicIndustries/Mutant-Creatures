* Migrate to the GTNH buildscript with Java 8 output, modern Java syntax support, and updated development tooling.
* Resolve AnimationAPI 1.2.4 through CurseMaven instead of bundling its source. Install AnimationAPI separately on both client and server.
* Generate the mod version from the build and configure Angelic Maven and release publishing.
* Add Catalogue Vintage artwork, license information, and an issue tracker link.
* Use the original CurseForge project image as the logo and a mutant-enderman screenshot as the Catalogue background.
* Include the upstream AGPLv3 license and update the README.
* Add a Chemical X mob list (`ChemicalXMobs`, flipped by `ChemicalXMobsIsWhitelist`). By default Chemical X only takes the mobs that have a mutant form, instead of killing any mob without a boss health bar.
* Feeding a creeper minion gunpowder makes it hiss contentedly (a CC0 recording), and feeding it TNT plays a short fuse.
* Text that was hardcoded in English now comes from the lang files: the creeper minion screen, the "Spawn" on spawn eggs, and a creeper minion's default name. Entities that had no name, such as the creeper minion egg, the mutant skeleton's scattered parts and thrown blocks, now have one in English and Chinese, so mods like WAILA show it. The spider pig is called Spider Pig instead of INCOMPLETE. Thanks to @Omgise for the report.
* Chemical X's skull spirit now shows its skulls on servers too. In singleplayer its particles no longer read the server's world from the client thread, which ArchaicFix warned about on every throw.
* The mutant zombie no longer floats a little above the ground: its bent legs ended about a tenth of a block short.
* Add the mutant husk: the mutant zombie in a husk's colours, spawning in deserts (`SpawnMutantHusk`). Its slam and leap always leave the target hungry. With Et Futurum Requiem, Chemical X turns a husk into one and its roar raises husks; existing configs need `etfuturum.husk` added to `ChemicalXMobs`.
* Add the hungry mutant zombie, for Special Mobs. Chemical X turns Special Mobs' hungry zombie into one, and it also spawns on its own (`SpawnHungryMutantZombie`). Its belly is eaten away down to the ribs and spine. It grabs its target, lifts it to its face and bites three times, eating food out of a player's inventory to heal, then flings it away. It walks over to food on the ground and eats it, even mid-fight if the food is close. What it eats falls out through its belly. Its roar raises Special Mobs' hungry zombies. It drops apples.
* Add the fire mutant zombie, for Special Mobs. Chemical X turns Special Mobs' fire zombie into one, and it also spawns on its own in the Overworld and the Nether (`SpawnFireMutantZombie`). It is charred with glowing lava cracks, always aflame and immune to fire. Its ground slam and leap set targets alight, it breathes a cone of fire at targets three to nine blocks away, and its roar raises Special Mobs' fire zombies. Water and rain hurt it and put out its breath; while it lies dead, water finishes it instead of fire. It drops coal.
* Add the brutish mutant zombie, for Special Mobs. Chemical X turns Special Mobs' brutish zombie into one, and it also spawns on its own (`SpawnBrutishMutantZombie`). It has the brutish zombie's two big teeth and holds the vanilla wooden sword, scaled up and glinting like the brutish zombie's enchanted one. Like the brutish zombie it has 10 extra armor, and it has 180 health. Its hits take 2 health past armor, as the brutish zombie's Pain sword does. A third of its attacks are a sword sweep that hits everything in the half circle in front of it for 10 damage and flings it away. Its roar raises Special Mobs' brutish zombies. It drops flint, and rarely an iron ingot.
* Add the fishing mutant zombie, for Special Mobs. Chemical X turns Special Mobs' fishing zombie into one, and it also spawns on its own (`SpawnFishingMutantZombie`). It is a plain mutant zombie holding the vanilla fishing rod, scaled up, and a little slower. When its target is 4 to 16 blocks away and in sight, it casts a hook every few seconds that bites for 2 damage and latches on, then yanks the target over to land just in front of it and slams the ground. A hook that lands on a block or in water is reeled back in, and creative players and mobs that shrug off the bite aren't hooked. Its roar raises Special Mobs' fishing zombies. It drops raw fish, and rarely a fishing rod.
* Add the plague mutant zombie, for Special Mobs. Chemical X turns Special Mobs' plague zombie into one, and it also spawns on its own (`SpawnPlagueMutantZombie`). It has the plague zombie's sickly skin and sores and is 30% faster than the mutant zombie. Each hit of its slam, leap or throw gives two doses of the plague zombie's plague: one of slowness, nausea, blindness, hunger, weakness or poison at random, each dose raising the effect a level up to the plague zombie's caps. When its target is 3 to 8 blocks away it retches onto the ground at the target's feet, leaving a plague cloud for 6 seconds that gives a dose each second to anything standing in it. Its roar raises Special Mobs' plague zombies. It drops mushrooms, and rarely a fermented spider eye.
* With Special Mobs, Chemical X turns its plain zombies, creepers, skeletons and endermen into the regular mutants. Special Mobs replaces the vanilla mobs with these by default, so before this Chemical X couldn't make the regular mutants at all.
* Existing configs need `SpecialMobs.HungryZombie`, `SpecialMobs.FireZombie`, `SpecialMobs.BrutishZombie`, `SpecialMobs.FishingZombie`, `SpecialMobs.PlagueZombie`, `SpecialMobs.SpecialZombie`, `SpecialMobs.SpecialCreeper`, `SpecialMobs.SpecialSkeleton` and `SpecialMobs.SpecialEnderman` added to `ChemicalXMobs` for Chemical X to take those mobs.
* Special Mobs zombies and Et Futurum husks raised by a mutant's roar act as its minions, as the mutant zombie's own do: they stay near it, take its target, ignore its ground slams and drop loot only a third of the time. Mixins into those mods add this, and it is saved with them.
* UniMixins is now required.
* Salamander is now required. The hungry mutant zombie is a GeckoLib model, driven by the mutant zombie's own animation code with GeckoLib clips for its own attacks.
